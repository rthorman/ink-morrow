Story-shaping follows your desired developments within continuity and boundaries. Living-world allows credible refusal and failure: asking a guard repeatedly is not new authority, but a verified warrant can change the answer. Both styles allow quiet cooperation and neither requires an avatar.

In Living-world, characters may knowingly address you, the reader. Rarely permits at most one address in six narrated passages; Freely allows it when fitting. Neither weakens resistance, reveals secrets or makes you an avatar.
