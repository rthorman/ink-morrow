# N1–N6 — source-grounded narrative drive and character consistency

Implemented on `recovery/narrative-drive`, based on B7 main `7ec3fa9`. Application
5.0.0/schema 29 stay unchanged. Delivery remains pending final exact-head checks
and actual integration. Current runtime/formats are the target; the owner has
explicitly waived backwards compatibility. Owner files/history remain protected.

## Implemented behavior

- **N1:** persistent branch truth is separate from bounded attention. Relationship,
  active-goal and commitment selection includes relevant actors and relationship
  targets. Exact source kind/field/quote/hash is recovered from immutable applicable
  fact/evidence records; initial facts and missing precision are labelled. Presence,
  possession, significance, knowledge, claims and events remain distinct.
- **N2:** complete effective cast/reference material remains supplied. Guidance
  preserves motives, voice, values, limits and authored willingness to bear costs.
  Existing character-design calls develop independent uses for interests after
  acceptance/rejection/success. Seed choices remain user-owned; drafts are unsaved
  until adoption. Existing references/accepted fiction are not rewritten.
- **N3:** a final next-move gate follows recent history and handoff in the existing
  two-message JSON request: current purpose, feasible method, observable action and
  consequence. Meaningful quiet, ordinary enjoyment and completion remain valid;
  no compulsory crisis, novelty quota, printed planning or player authorship.
- **N4:** prior structured outcomes are projected from their own source beats,
  including refusals preceding changed grounds/acceptance. Meaningful unstructured
  consequences use existing evidenced fact/relationship effects; no regex semantic
  extractor, tactic blacklist or blanket prohibition on purposeful persistence.
- **N5:** compact chapter/episode context reads the applicable editorial revision
  and indexed anchors. Handoff references changed records, established scene,
  active intentions, unresolved commitments and recorded milestones. An empty next
  chapter is recognized without inventing a time/place transition or achievement.
- **N6:** deterministic context/request/branch tests plus the separate bounded
  opt-in rolling-chapter evaluation protocol are supplied. It is not paid-test
  authorization or evidence of improved real-model literary quality.

All projections are read-only. Source history is retained independently of working
selection. At most twelve structured attempt states are expanded, with relevant
challenge filtering when applicable. Current sources are rebuilt after a save copy
rather than accepted as new saved authority. All added bytes enter existing
capacity/consent accounting; an unfit protected context is rejected before purchase.
One/four/six existing call ceilings and default quality Off are unchanged. Review
copy explicitly discloses exact evidence, private assertions/submitted input, prior
outcomes and handoff. This may increase input cost within the reviewed bound.

## Limits and evidence

Guidance is not a semantic guarantee. A free-form refusal not retained as an
applicable fact may fall outside the bounded recent window. Existing generated
facts or summaries can still be semantically mistaken; exact quotation alone does
not prove the claim. Optional review is fallible. No new model calls, silent
retries, source rewriting or paid evaluation were added.

Ten new deterministic cases cover source retention beyond twelve passages, full
character material, ownership/knowledge, intention/assertion qualification,
relationship-target selection, sibling/future isolation, old refusal then changed
grounds, empty-next-chapter handoff, quiet Follow/three commitments, current-format
source remapping/capacity rejection and unsaved reference design/idempotency.

passed 344/344 across 39 suites. Lint, brand, release identities and deferred-PDF
