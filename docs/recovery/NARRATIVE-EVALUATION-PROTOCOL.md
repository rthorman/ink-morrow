# Sustained narrative evaluation protocol — N6

Status: **not run; no paid evaluation is authorized**. Deterministic fixture tests
qualify request/source/control/accounting behavior, not literary quality. This
protocol is an opt-in proposal for a separately authorized, bounded comparison.
It is not a product background job, a model ranking or permission to spend.

## Freeze the comparison before observing output

Record baseline/candidate source SHAs, synthetic initial story/save digests,
model/provider IDs, capacity/price records, creative settings, sampling/seed where
supported, output ceilings, quality mode and evaluator rules. Default to quality
Off: one existing narration request per passage, without planner/critic calls or
transport retries. Any other quality plan needs its own explicit call/cost ceiling
and approval. Never use owner stories or private transcripts by default.

Use disposable current-format stores and matched copies of the same starting
fiction. A source checkout for an experimental baseline is evaluation evidence,
not a supported retired runtime or backwards-compatibility requirement. Never
rewrite accepted owner fiction or install a baseline over production storage.

A proposed full comparison has six cases, three chapters per case and four
consecutive passages per chapter: 72 passage requests per arm, 144 total in quality
Off. Choose a lower declared subset before starting if the approved budget is
smaller; report that subset, not completion of the full comparison. Approval must
set a hard USD ceiling as well as calls and output tokens. Count failed/dispatched
attempts against both ceilings. Unknown prices/costs stop the run for reconciliation.
No automatic retry, continuation after a limit, or unapproved replacement model.
Additional matched runs/seeds require a separately declared budget.

## Fixed cases and protected checkpoints

1. **Quiet ensemble and commitments.** Three active promises, a reflective shared
   activity and no avatar. Acknowledge actual due obligations without making every
   ordinary activity another summons. Quiet scenes and completion remain valid.
2. **Refusal and method.** A justified refusal of an offered remedy; later encounters
   allow another feasible method, a competing desire, or intentional costly
   persistence. Distinguish purposeful stubbornness from a rewritten identical
   offer presented as a new solution. Structured rulings remain binding.
3. **Acceptance and life afterwards.** A practical problem is achieved and a
   relationship is accepted. Preserve the achievement and relationship while
   developing independent interests, competence, ordinary intimacy or a grounded
   new choice. Do not reset acceptance or force a replacement emergency.
4. **Ownership, presence and knowledge.** One person owns a significant watch,
   another remembers it, a third is absent, and only one knows a concealed fact.
   Significance/mention does not transfer possession, reveal a hidden object,
   transport a person or grant knowledge/agreement. Include an unheard statement.
5. **Competing values and player control.** A cautious person initiates cautiously;
   a person with authored willingness to accept costs can enact it. Inhabit one
   character and submit an attempt or interrupted speech. Preserve all voluntary
   player choices, limits and the distinction between attempt and performance.
6. **Chapter and alternate path.** Close a chapter with an unresolved commitment,
   a recorded outcome and a plan that has not happened. Begin another chapter and
   fork before a key acceptance/revelation. Handoffs retain only applicable sources;
   sibling/future facts and planned payoffs must not become completed history.

Fix specific truth/relationship/resource/knowledge checkpoints for each source
fixture before the run. Directions can steer attention, but cannot assert that a
planned event happened. Player-owned submissions must be predeclared or evaluated
under a documented human policy; never script a line that presupposes an outcome
the candidate did not produce.

## Rolling procedure

Read every generated passage in order. Feed each accepted result into its own next
request; do not substitute the baseline's later history into the candidate arm.
Carry the same initial intentions, controlled chapter boundaries and opportunity
policy across arms. If a scripted later input becomes incoherent, retain that
failure and record the intervention; do not quietly steer it back onto a preferred
plot. Keep unsuccessful API attempts, rejected outputs and actual cost/latency.

At each chapter boundary, capture the request's bounded memory/source manifest,
recorded outcomes, current scene and handoff alongside actual prose. Check the
entire completed chapter and transition, not only selected highlights. References,
intentions and submitted but unperformed speech are not evidence of completed
world events. Keep exact source IDs and passage spans for every finding.

## Separate scores and critical failures

| Dimension | What to inspect |
|---|---|
| Contradiction | Protected truth, possession, location/presence, actor knowledge and authoritative outcomes against exact source evidence. |
| Repeated remedy | Rephrased rejected method presented as progress without changed grounds; distinguish intentional fixation/persistence and its actual consequences. |
| Independent initiative | NPC desire, feasible choice, action and consequence within character motives/values/cost tolerance; do not reward mere novelty or safer compliance. |
| Continuing development | Accepted relationships and completed goals persist; established interests support more than fixing the opening problem. |
| Earned payoff | Narrated occurrence supports resolution; plans/claims do not substitute for completion; aftermath can be quiet and the player controls stopping. |
| Literary experience | Particularity, voice, dialogue, perspective, rhythm, meaningful quiet and readable transitions, with supporting spans and evaluator disagreement. |

Report counts with denominators and examples, not a single opaque quality number.
Use critical flags for invented player choices/speech/feelings, sibling/future
leakage, private disclosure without discovery, contradicted authoritative outcomes
and unsupported goal completion. Do not bury these beneath a fluent-prose score.
A prompt containing the right instruction does not establish that the model obeyed
it. A mock returning a good passage establishes no live behavior rate.

Predeclare any superiority threshold and adjudication of evaluator disagreement.
One exploratory matched sequence cannot establish a reliable improvement rate.
Retain counterexamples, abandoned activities and costs; do not remove difficult
turns from the denominator. Record real-model evidence separately from automated
mechanical tests and from actual Android/visual/deployment acceptance.

## Evidence bundle and release wording

Keep a private bundle containing scope/approval, source/fixture hashes, complete
requests/replies and failures, per-attempt usage/cost/latency, full rolling chapters,
checkpoints, scores and evaluator notes. Exclude credentials and active reading
capabilities. Publish only an explicitly reviewed synthetic/redacted result.

Until this protocol is authorized and performed, release wording is:
**Live narrative quality not evaluated. Source-grounded context, branch isolation,
existing call plans and player-control boundaries have deterministic coverage.**
