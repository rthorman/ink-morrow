# InkMorrow 5.0 — limits and qualification

These are current product boundaries and qualification limits, not promises
that every model, platform or long-running story has been field-tested.

- New product, fresh data: no 4.x database, manuscript-archive or character-template
  import. Historical files are refused, not migrated or deleted.
- No manual prose editor, old writing rooms, reusable catalogue, narration,
  audiobook or public snapshot-sharing workflow.
- New passages and Ask require a configured provider. Local reading, correction,
  paths, recaps, uploads, saves and books remain available without AI purchases.
- Models can contradict state, leak fictional secrets in prose, capitulate too
  easily or refuse justified cooperation. Structured challenges protect recorded
  rulings, not arbitrary dialogue. No live-model ranking is certified.
- Optional reviewers add cost/latency and can miss problems or reject acceptable
  prose. At most one repair is allowed; failed work can still cost money.
- One-owner access control is not disk encryption or a hosted multi-user service.
  Saves contain private state and are unencrypted. Protect backups and keys.
- Cast is bounded to 24, paths to 40, current illustrated moments to 200 and
  retained story assets to 400. The 128-fact working set is not a lifetime limit.
- Saves are bounded to 10,000 moments, 64 MB compressed and 128 MB expanded;
  conservative preflight can reject unusually large graphs. Keep cold backups.
- Browser coverage uses desktop/mobile Chrome and fixture data. Other engines,
  real-device performance and live provider behaviour need separate qualification.
- EPUB receiving apps differ in support for fixed-layout image pages mixed with
  reflowable prose. Inspect a representative exported book in your chosen reader.

See the [User Guide](../../pdf/Ink-Morrow-5.0-User-Guide.pdf),
[Operations handbook](../../pdf/Ink-Morrow-5.0-Operations-and-Recovery-Handbook.pdf)
and [release evidence](README.md). Code integration is not deployment;
